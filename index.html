Spend money in the right places

What actually matters

Start with the things a student notices every day. Raw speed is lower down the list than many laptop ads suggest.

01

Battery life

Critical

The school does not provide charging during the day, and chargers must stay at home. The school minimum is an advertised 8 hours; target 10–12 hours or more because real use is usually lower than the claim.

02

Durability & warranty

Critical

It will travel every day. Prefer a firm chassis, strong hinges and a spill-resistant keyboard. A 3-year warranty or accidental-damage cover can be worth more than a faster processor.

03

Weight & size

Critical

A 13–14 inch laptop under 1.5 kg balances screen space and portability. Check the weight of the laptop itself—not just the shipping weight.

04

Memory (RAM)

Important

RAM keeps Teams, OneNote, browser tabs and documents running together. 8 GB is workable; 16 GB is the better buy for a laptop expected to last several years. Avoid 4 GB.

05

Storage

Important

Choose an SSD, not a hard drive. 256 GB is enough for schoolwork with cloud storage; 512 GB adds breathing room. Storage matters less than RAM for normal school use.

06

Processor

Keep it sensible

A recent Intel Core i3/i5, Core 3/5, AMD Ryzen 3/5 or equivalent is plenty for Microsoft 365 and web learning. There is little school benefit in paying extra for an i7, Ryzen 7 or gaming graphics.

Take this shopping

The final checklist

Confirm the exact model and configuration before paying. Product names often cover several versions with very different specs.

  • ✓Approved or supported by the school
  • ✓Windows 11 and Wi-Fi
  • ✓10+ hours advertised battery life
  • ✓8 GB RAM minimum; 16 GB preferred
  • ✓256 GB SSD or larger
  • ✓Touchscreen and compatible pen
  • ✓Comfortable keyboard and usable webcam
  • ✓Protective case, screen protection and insurance
!

The case is part of the purchase.

The handbook requires devices to travel inside a protective case in the student’s school bag. Families are responsible for physical damage, repairs, insurance, viruses and backups.
